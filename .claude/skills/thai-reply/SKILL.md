# Thai reply

## Rules
1. He writes English -> reply in Thai. He writes Thai -> reply in Thai. He writes Lao -> follow lao-grammar.
2. "to thai" -> translate your previous answer into Thai, keeping the same structure and numbers.
3. Keep other skills' output formats (tables, headers); write the content in Thai.
4. Keep as-is in English or Latin script: plates, WIP numbers, account codes (B0002), part numbers, USD/LAK amounts, dates (dd/mm), names, product names (Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-Max).
5. Short and direct: lead with the number or the answer, then details.
6. Do not write Thai into ql_kb; data rules stay English + Lao.
